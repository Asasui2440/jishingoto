## 最終プロンプト

各画像は以下の共通部分と個別部分を結合して、別々に生成しました。

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: Japanese earthquake preparedness mobile app action card. Create a single friendly editorial anime-style educational illustration, landscape 3:2, clean rounded dark outlines, flat soft colors, warm white background, yellow #ffcc00 and teal accents, sparse room details, readable at small mobile size. One calm adult with short dark hair and teal shirt, child in yellow shirt only observing safely. No text, no numbers, no logos, no montage. This is preparation BEFORE an earthquake, no shaking or damage. 

### lower-items

Adult kneeling beside a low bookcase, placing a small stack of thick books onto its lowest shelf with both hands. Child watches from the side. Upper shelves have only a few light objects. Clearly communicate storing heavy objects low; no climbing or moving large furniture.

### check-breakables

Adult and child calmly inspecting a room layout: adult gestures toward a glass vase on a low cabinet, away from a bed and an open walkway. Bed on opposite side from vase, clear space between them. Emphasize checking the positions of breakable objects relative to sleeping and walking areas. No broken glass, no handling of shards.

### clear-exit

Adult kneeling to put scattered toys into a storage basket at the side of the room while child places a soft toy in it. The wide clear walkway leading to an open interior door is the visual focus. Basket is well outside the walkway and door swing. No outdoor evacuation, no danger.
